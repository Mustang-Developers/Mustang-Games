About Us

The NHS Unity Game Design Club (Mustang Developers) uses Unity to develop games from scratch. They plan, design, create graphics and audio, and program all parts of the game, and anyone can contribute. To get in contact or ask any questions, email unitygamedevnhs@gmail.com!
